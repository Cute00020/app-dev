## Green Book
# Overview
 Green Book is a 2018 American biographical comedy-drama film directed by Peter Farrelly.
 Starring Viggo Mortensen and Mahershala Ali, 
 the film is inspired by the true story of a 1962 tour of the Deep South by 
 African-American pianist Don Shirley and Italian-American bouncer and 
 later actor Frank "Tony Lip" Vallelonga, who served as Shirley's driver and bodyguard. 
 # Key Details
 1. Genre: Drama, Action, Adventure
 2. Film
 3. Broadcast Period:  September 11, 2018 (2018-09-11)
# Plot Summary 
Don hires Tony on the strength of his references. 
They embark with plans to return to New York City on Christmas Eve.
Don's record label gives Tony a copy of The Negro Motorist Green Book,
a guide for African American travelers that contains the addresses of 
those motels, restaurants, and filling stations that would serve them in the Jim Crow South.
# Characters
Viggo Mortensen as Tony Lip
Mahershala Ali as Dr. Donald Shirley
# Themes 
Green Book are friendship and acceptance,
focusing on how two very different men from different 
backgrounds develop a strong bond and overcome prejudices together.
